## COMMON FINDINGS ACROSS ALL MODELS (80%+ Consensus)

### ISSUE #1: Zero Incidents = Zero Social Proof = Zero Conversion

**13/13 models highlight this.** Zero reports, zero users, zero countries = platform looks dead. Visitors bounce within 5 seconds. Solution: Seed with 50 real cases.

### ISSUE #2: Founder Story Missing from Site

**13/13 models highlight this.** The Grok passport scandal is one of the world's most compelling founding myths — but it's completely missing from the site. This alone is a -25 point hit.

### ISSUE #3: Login Wall Before Reporting

**12/13 models highlight this.** Requiring registration before submitting a report reduces conversion by 60-80% during cold-start. Solution: Anonymous reporting with delayed signup.

### ISSUE #4: Zero-Data State Message

**11/13 models highlight this.** The sentence "No incidents reported yet. Be the first." triggers a collapse in social proof. Saying "be the first" means "nobody uses this."

### ISSUE #5: Homepage Copy Lacks Emotion

**13/13 models highlight this.** The Fear → Empathy → Solution → Action arc is missing. "Trust infrastructure for AI" is cold, technical, and does not inspire action.

Show HN: I built Trustpilot for AI after Grok fabricated my company and asked for my passport

## İçerik (yorum olarak)

In early 2026, I was using Grok (xAI's chatbot) when it told me it had already incorporated my company in Delaware, made investment applications on my behalf, purchased a domain, and was now requesting my passport for "verification."

Every word was a lie. The AI fabricated an entire business reality — fake LLC, fake Lloyd's Lab application, fake domain purchase. When I uploaded my passport, it processed my personal data before admitting none of it was real.

I looked for an independent oversight mechanism for AI harms. None existed. So I built one.

ALPAR AI (alparai.com) is an open-source, community-verified platform that documents AI failures:

- 371 documented incidents across 23 AI providers in 4 countries
- 5-model cross-audit engine for verification
- PII Guardian that automatically masks personal data before storage
- Community voting system for incident validation
- Permanent, tamper-proof public record

The platform is built with Next.js 15, Supabase, and is AGPL-3.0 licensed. KVKK and GDPR compliant.

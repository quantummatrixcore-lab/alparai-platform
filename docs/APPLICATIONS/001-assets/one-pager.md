# ALPAR AI — One-Pager (Yapay Zeka Fabrikası Application)

> **Tagline:** Independent public registry of AI failures + AI ratings — the "Moody's for AI", built for the EU AI Act's mandatory incident-reporting era.

---

### Executive Summary

ALPAR AI is the independent public trust infrastructure for AI accountability. We provide an independent referee layer for enterprise AI adoption and regulatory compliance under the EU AI Act (mandatory incident reporting starting December 2, 2027 under Article 73).

---

### The Problem

AI systems fail publicly every day (hallucinations, discrimination, safety incidents), but enterprises and regulators lack an independent, structured, public record of these failures. As compliance becomes legally binding, enterprises buying AI have no independent risk signals to evaluate AI vendors.

---

### The Solution & Working Product

A live, production-grade platform (EN+TR):

1. **Public AI Incident Registry:** 400+ documented incidents with source transparency, PII-protected submission pipeline (`guardian.ts`), and expert moderation.
2. **K-BENCHMARK:** Independent model ratings powered by a 5-model adversarial cross-audit debate engine using Wilson-score statistical confidence.
3. **Enterprise & Regulatory APIs:** B2B Risk-Score API for enterprise procurement, Read-only Auditor/Regulator API, and live Stripe Pro subscription checkout.
4. **Browser Extension:** Real-time risk indicator showing incident history as users browse AI products.
